Privacy notice

This notice explains, as required by Art. 13 GDPR, what happens to personal data when you open this page. It is short because the page does very little: no cookies, no login, no forms, no analytics.

What this page loads

Nothing from third-party servers. Fonts, images and stylesheet sit on the same server as the page itself. There are no cookies, no analytics, no Google Analytics, no advertising or social media widgets, and no Google Fonts.

That is a check in the build rather than a promise: if any of the pages contains an address pointing at a third-party server, the build stops and nothing is published. There is currently no exception to this rule.

Hosting and server logs

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open it, your browser transmits technically necessary data to their servers, including your IP address, date, time, the address requested, browser type as well as operating system. GitHub stores this access data in log files. We have no access to it as well as no way to analyse it.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering the page securely, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The transfer to the USA relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, in which GitHub participates. Details are in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contacting us

If you send us an e-mail, we process your details in order to answer. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where the enquiry concerns a possible engagement, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. We keep the message for as long as it is needed to handle the matter or as long as statutory retention periods apply, then delete it.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), as well as the right to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (Art. 21). An e-mail to jens.laufer@solytics.de is enough.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, Promenade 27, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

No automated decision-making

There is no automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
